# Eight-week shadow-mode pilot and sample SOW

## Scope

One domain, one bounded recurring decision, synthetic or lawfully supplied client data, and a frozen classical baseline. The service produces recommendations for human review only.

## Timeline

1. Weeks 1–2: data contract, provenance, current-process baseline.
2. Weeks 3–4: formulation, checker, benchmark, failure review.
3. Weeks 5–6: shadow operation, overrides, latency, reliability.
4. Weeks 7–8: value evidence, integration estimate, proceed/pivot/stop review.

## Acceptance

The signed SOW must set feasibility rate, objective improvement, solve-time percentile, data-error rate, human override rate, integration effort, cost per decision, and reliability thresholds. Realized KPI remains the client's measurement responsibility.

## Responsibilities

The client owns lawful data, constraint validation, human decisions, operational safety, and realized KPI measurement. The provider owns reproducibility, checker results, uncertainty, incident escalation, and rollback support.

## Termination and rollback

Stop or roll back when feasibility, data quality, latency, integration effort, cost, or human-override thresholds fail; when the decision owner withdraws approval; or when evidence no longer matches the agreed scope. No auto-actuation is permitted.
