# Portfolio allocation formulation

- Binary variable: synthetic asset selected.
- Hard constraints: cardinality, minimum synthetic return, and sector exposure count.
- Objective: minimize synthetic risk minus twice synthetic return.
- Native baseline: CP-SAT integer selection.
- Heuristic: risk-return ranking with exposure guardrails.
- QUBO: bounded binary-selection diagnostic only.

All assets, returns, and risks are fictional. This laboratory is not investment advice and does not generate transactions or recommendations for real securities.
