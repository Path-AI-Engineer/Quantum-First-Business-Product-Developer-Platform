# Service charter

## Offer

`Optimization Opportunity Assessment + Pilot Design` translates a bounded operational decision into a verifiable mathematical model, establishes a strong classical baseline, models conditional value, and recommends one of four outcomes:

- `OPTIMIZE_NOW_CLASSICAL`
- `EXPERIMENT_QUANTUM_READY`
- `DATA_OR_PROCESS_FIRST`
- `DO_NOT_PILOT`

The service sells diagnosis and evidence, not a “magic solver.” Infeasibility, insufficient data, no economic case, and no quantum advantage are valid results.

## Delivery phases

1. Discovery and data readiness.
2. Problem formulation and independent feasibility checker.
3. Classical benchmark under a frozen budget.
4. Optional bounded quantum-readiness experiment.
5. Parametric value and integration model.
6. Shadow-mode pilot design and decision.

## Non-claims

- Synthetic fixtures are not client outcomes.
- Value ranges are not realized savings or financial advice.
- QUBO compatibility is not quantum advantage.
- The prototype does not actuate schedules, routes, or allocations.
- No cloud or quantum-hardware workload is required or executed.
