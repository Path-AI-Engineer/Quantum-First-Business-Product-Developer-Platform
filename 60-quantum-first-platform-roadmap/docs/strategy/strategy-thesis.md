# Strategy thesis

## Where to play

The Year-1 wedge is enterprise PQC readiness plus classical-first optimization assessment, supported by a governed developer sandbox and evidence plane. These offers can create measurable value with currently available technology.

## How to win

Compete on auditable decisions, provider-neutral contracts, explicit baselines, and controlled evidence. Do not compete on unverified quantum advantage or long-range dates.

## Sequence

1. Commit to PQC inventory, IAM, evidence, observability, and authorized assessments.
2. Productize only after adoption, reliability, economics, and evidence gates pass.
3. Preserve quantum research as real options with trigger, expiry, carrying cost, classical baseline, and kill path.

## Anti-thesis and non-goals

- A hardware calendar is not a strategy.
- Multi-cloud and Kubernetes are not goals; they require workload and TCO evidence.
- This project does not claim revenue, customers, funding, board approval, quantum advantage, or a financial forecast.
- The Control Tower never executes cloud, financial, staffing, or product actions.
