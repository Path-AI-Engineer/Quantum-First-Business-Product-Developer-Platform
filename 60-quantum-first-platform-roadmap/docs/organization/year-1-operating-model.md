# Year-1 organization and cadence

Use a small T-shaped team with explicit product, platform, security/PQC, optimization/data/AI, research, SRE/FinOps, enterprise success, and legal/partner ownership. Specialist capacity is contracted or partnered until a workload gate justifies hiring. On-call begins only with a production obligation.

Monthly reviews inspect metrics, spend, risks, and contradictory evidence. Quarterly reviews evaluate entry, continue, scale, hold, and kill gates; rebalance the portfolio; record dissent; and update the capability graph. Bus factor and learning plans are reviewed with ownership and succession notes.
