# PQC, AI, and quantum governance

## PQC track

Maintain cryptographic inventory, data-lifetime priority, vendor readiness, lab/stage/rollout evidence, standards watch, and exception ownership. NIST published the principal PQC standards in 2024 and recommends migration activity now; the roadmap therefore treats PQC as a Year-1 obligation.

## AI track

Maintain a model inventory, use-case/risk class, data boundary, evaluation, human oversight, incident/rollback procedure, and provider exit. Outcomes align to NIST AI RMF while recording that version 1.0 is under revision as of the dated source profile.

## Quantum track

Every experiment records a classical baseline, provider/job/cost envelope, resource estimate, no-advantage result, maturity trigger, approval, and research-to-product handoff. Calendar-only commitments are prohibited.
