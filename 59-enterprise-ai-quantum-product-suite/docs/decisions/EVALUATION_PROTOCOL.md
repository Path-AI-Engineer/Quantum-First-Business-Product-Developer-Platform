# Frozen evaluation protocol

The final deterministic campaign contains exactly 687 cases:

- 240 authorization and tenant isolation cases;
- 120 contract and event compatibility cases;
- 90 cross-module journey cases;
- 72 AI grounding, injection, and abstention cases;
- 60 policy and approval cases;
- 45 fault and recovery cases;
- 30 report reconciliation cases;
- 18 persona usability tasks;
- 12 packaging and pilot decision scenarios.

Acceptance requires zero cross-tenant exposure, unauthorized action, replay, or secrets; every recommendation has evidence, uncertainty, and a decision owner; AI has no side effects; reports reconcile; correlations survive journeys; each module retains a standalone path; commercial claims remain hypotheses; and the candidate/prototype/production gap is explicit.

The deterministic evaluator is a reference protocol and fixture-level demonstration. It is not an external security assessment, customer acceptance, or production certification.
