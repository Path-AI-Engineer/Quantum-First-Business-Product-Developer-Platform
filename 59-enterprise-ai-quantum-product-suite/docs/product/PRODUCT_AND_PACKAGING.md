# Product hierarchy, pilots, and commercial hypotheses

## Suite thesis

The wedge is a governed decision, not quantum compute. An organization can enter through a standalone module, preserve evidence and policy semantics, and expand only after an observed usage or outcome signal.

| Package | Included capability | Primary buyer | Value unit hypothesis | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | inventory, evidence, policy, audit | CISO / platform lead | governed resources | hypothesis |
| Readiness | PQC findings and migration portfolio | crypto lead | assessed assets | hypothesis |
| Optimization | intake, baseline, pilot dossier | COO / science lead | evaluated opportunities | hypothesis |
| Platform | sandbox capabilities and developer contracts | CTO | approved sandbox jobs | hypothesis |
| Enterprise Suite | combined modules and managed governance | executive sponsor | governed decisions | hypothesis |

Pricing is not validated. Quantum compute, if ever connected, must remain a visible passthrough. Professional services effort and margin are not represented as software revenue. No ROI estimate is a realized outcome.

## Land and expand

1. Land with a synthetic inventory or one controlled opportunity assessment.
2. Prove evidence quality, authorization, adoption, and decision cycle time.
3. Expand to another module only when a recorded success gate passes.
4. Kill or hold a pilot when safety, evidence, value, or operability thresholds fail.

## Pilot ladder

| Stage | Entry | Success gate | Kill gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | named owner and synthetic scope | evidence completeness >= 95% | unknown owner or data boundary |
| Assessment | approved protocol | baseline reproducible | unreconciled data |
| Sandbox | scoped approval and budget | artifact and trace reproducible | policy bypass or leakage |
| Decision | human review | assumptions and dissent recorded | recommendation treated as authorization |

## Service/software boundary and partners

Software provides catalog, evidence, policy, workflows, reports, and local adapters. Services include discovery, domain formulation, migration planning, and enablement. Potential integrations (identity, ticketing, providers, storage) are a partner map, not implemented partnerships.

## Customer-success scorecard (synthetic pilot)

Measure active governed users, evidence completeness, median approval cycle, report reconciliation, support recovery, and decisions with an owner. Never substitute API calls, generated summaries, or compute volume for business outcomes.
