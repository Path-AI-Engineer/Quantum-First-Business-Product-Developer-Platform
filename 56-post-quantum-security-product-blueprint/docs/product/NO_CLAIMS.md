# No-claims policy — Day 1562

Status: candidate for human review, 2026-09-14. Apply these checks to future
UI copy, API responses, assessment reports and release notes.

| Avoid claiming | Allowed evidence-bounded wording |
| --- | --- |
| “This asset is quantum safe” | “The recorded mechanisms and controls within this scope were observed as stated; unknowns and implementation risk remain.” |
| “PQC readiness/compliance certified” | “This fixture assessment identifies evidence and decisions requiring an authorized cryptographic or compliance review.” |
| “A cryptographically relevant quantum computer will arrive in year X” | “Long-lived data and migration lead times justify planning without an arrival-date prediction.” |
| “Every RSA/ECC use is equally urgent” | “Priority depends on exposure, data lifetime, criticality, dependencies, replaceability and evidence confidence.” |
| “NIST sets a universal corporate deadline” | “The applicable transition schedule must be checked by jurisdiction, sector, use and document status; IR 8547 remains an initial public draft as checked on 2026-09-14.” |
| “The product has completed a migration” | “The product proposes a human-owned wave and records acceptance evidence; it does not change systems.” |
| “Vendor-declared support is verified” | “Vendor declaration is a separate knowledge state until independent evidence is linked.” |
| “An empty or negative scan proves absence” | “No observation within a bounded source and scope is not evidence of absence.” |

Every substantive finding must carry a source type, observed time, scope,
parser/tool version, evidence hash, knowledge state, confidence, freshness
and limitations. Reports must retain contradictions and exceptions. Production
security, tenant isolation and regulatory assertions require tests and human
review beyond the local prototype.
