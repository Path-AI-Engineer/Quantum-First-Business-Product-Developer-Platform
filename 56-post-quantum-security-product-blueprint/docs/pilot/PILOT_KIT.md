# Six-week pilot kit — proposal for review, not a signed engagement

Entry conditions: named customer sponsor, lawful data-processing basis, agreed
scope and retention, approved threat model, real authentication, production
isolation and explicit consent to process customer inventories. None are met
by this synthetic lab. No customer data should be uploaded to the prototype.

| Week | Joint activity | Measurable evidence | Exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scope two source types, asset owners and data classes | Signed scope and baseline denominator | Sponsor approval |
| 2 | Import offline inventory and verify sample | Coverage and schema rejection log | No critical parser issue |
| 3 | Triage findings with crypto architect | Precision/recall on reviewed sample, conflict resolution | Findings trace to evidence |
| 4 | Build one laboratory migration wave | Owner, dependency, lead-time and acceptance ledger | Change board approval |
| 5 | Produce executive and technical reports | Reconciled source/version totals | Risk reviewer acceptance |
| 6 | Compare posture and decide | Unknown reduction, task success and incident register | Proceed/pivot/stop decision |

Metrics must show numerator, denominator, missingness and confidence interval
where relevant: inventory coverage; evidence completeness; confirmed finding
precision/recall; median triage time; unknown reduction; owner assignment;
report reproducibility; task completion; security/privacy incidents. A metric
without a baseline or verified sample is **not measured**.

Rollback/termination: the pilot can stop on a privacy incident, failed tenant
isolation, misleading finding, unverified claim or revoked sponsor approval.
Exports and retained evidence require a documented deletion/return process.
This is a sample statement of work outline, not legal advice or a customer
contract. Responsibilities: customer owns source accuracy and change approval;
product team owns import controls, audit, reproducibility and support; assessor
checks cryptographic interpretation.
